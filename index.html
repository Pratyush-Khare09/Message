Meri Chhoti Bauaa,

I know the mistakes i have done.

Galti se hua, but ye galti se bhi nahi hona chahiye tha. Dobara wali cheez literally mere mind me aayi hi nahi bilkul. Par yaar is baar ho gayi, aur ab mujhe poora realize hai ki meri wo mistake kitni badi thi.

Aur is baar please maaf kar do, aur bas ek baar aur apne is bewakoof bhaiya ko mauka do.

"Yaar behna, tum toh meri chhoti behna ho na yaar... aise door na ho."

I'm not asking you to forget everything right away.
